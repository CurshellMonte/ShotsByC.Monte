ABOUT ME

In 2023, my journey into photography began unexpectedly. I attended an event where someone I knew was taking photographs. At the time, I never imagined that simply watching someone work with a camera would spark something inside me.

One day, I was given the opportunity to hold and use the camera. What started as a simple joke quickly turned into curiosity. I began taking pictures, experimenting with different angles, and enjoying the process more than I expected.

From that moment, I started developing a genuine interest in photography. I realized that photography was not only about taking pictures. It was about capturing emotions, personalities, special moments, and memories that people can keep forever.

Since then, I have continued learning, practicing, and improving my skills. Every photoshoot has taught me something new, and every experience has helped me become more confident and creative behind the camera.

Photography has become something I truly enjoy, and I want to continue growing while creating meaningful and high-quality images for others.

What began as a simple joke and a borrowed camera in 2023 has developed into a dream that I am determined to pursue.
